About Numerology

Numerology is the study of the mystical relationship between numbers and living things. It asserts that numbers have a divine, mystical connection to events and that each number vibrates with its own energy.

The most common system is Pythagorean numerology, which assigns numerical values to letters:

  • A, J, S = 1
  • B, K, T = 2
  • C, L, U = 3
  • D, M, V = 4
  • E, N, W = 5
  • F, O, X = 6
  • G, P, Y = 7
  • H, Q, Z = 8
  • I, R = 9

To calculate your Expression Number (also called Destiny Number):

  1. Write out your full name as it appears on your birth certificate.
  2. Assign each letter its numerical value.
  3. Add up all the numbers.
  4. Reduce the sum to a single digit by adding the digits together repeatedly, unless you get 11 or 22 (master numbers).

Similarly, the Soul Urge Number (Heart's Desire) uses only the vowels, and the Personality Number uses only the consonants.
