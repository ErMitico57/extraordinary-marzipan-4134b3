About

A small project
about a big number

Your Life in Numbers exists for a single, quiet reason: to make the time you've already lived feel real again.

The idea

We move through our days counting almost nothing. The heart beats, the lungs fill, the planet turns, and none of it asks for our attention. This little site takes one detail you know by heart — the moment you were born — and translates it into the totals that have been quietly adding up ever since: every breath, every heartbeat, every full moon you've stood beneath, and the silent millions of miles you've drifted through space without moving an inch on your own.

It's meant to be a small jolt of perspective and wonder, the kind of thing you read, smile at, and send to someone you love.

Where the numbers come from

Nothing here is guesswork dressed up as magic. Each total is built from well-documented human averages — a resting heart rate of about 72 beats a minute, roughly 16 breaths a minute, close to a third of life spent asleep — and from fixed astronomical facts, like the 29.53-day lunar cycle and the speed of our solar system through the galaxy. You can read the full breakdown on the home page.

Because these are averages, your real figures will be a little higher or lower. A calm heart beats slower over a lifetime; a restless sleeper dreams more. We've chosen accuracy over flattery, and honesty over false precision.

A note on privacy

The whole calculation runs inside your own browser. The birth date and time you enter are never uploaded, stored, or shared — they exist only on your screen and vanish the moment you leave. You can read more in our Privacy Policy.

One gentle reminder

This is a playful tool, not a health instrument. The numbers are there to delight and to give you pause — never to inform a medical or personal decision. Take them in the spirit they're offered: as poetry with a little arithmetic underneath.
