Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information is involved when you use Your Life in Numbers (the "Site"), how it is handled, and the choices you have. By using the Site, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Who we are

Your Life in Numbers is a small, independent web project that turns a date of birth into a set of estimated life statistics. You can reach us any time through our Contact page.

2. The information you enter

To show your results, the Site asks for your first name, last name, date of birth, and (optionally) time of birth. This information is processed entirely within your own browser, on your own device. It is used only to calculate your results in the moment, and it is never transmitted to us, stored on any server, saved in a database, or shared with anyone. When you close or refresh the page, it is gone.

3. Cookies and advertising

The Site is supported by advertising served through Google AdSense. To make this work, Google and its partners use cookies and similar technologies.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your previous visits to this Site or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this Site and/or other sites on the internet.
  • You can opt out of personalised advertising at any time by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising at aboutads.info.

For more detail on how Google uses data from sites that use its services, see Google's "How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services" page.

4. Consent for visitors in Europe (GDPR)

If you visit from the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, you will be shown a consent message before non-essential cookies are used, allowing you to consent, refuse, or manage your choices. We rely on a Google-certified consent management platform for this. You can change or withdraw your consent at any time through the privacy or consent settings presented on the Site. It is as easy to withdraw consent as it is to give it.

5. Analytics

The Site itself does not set its own analytics cookies and does not build a personal profile of you. Any data collection beyond your in-browser calculation comes from the advertising services described above.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal data held about you, and to object to or restrict certain processing. Because the birth details you enter are never stored by us, there is nothing on our side to retrieve or delete. For any data held by advertising partners, please use the opt-out tools linked above, or contact those providers directly. You may also contact us with any question.

7. Children

The Site is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review it periodically.

9. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please reach out through our Contact page.
