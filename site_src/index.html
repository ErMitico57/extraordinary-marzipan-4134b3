How it works

You give us one thing — the moment you were born — and everything else unfolds from there. The instant you press Reveal my life, your browser measures the exact time that has passed since your birth, down to the second, and multiplies it by the steady rhythms of the human body and the motion of the planet beneath you.

It all happens right inside your browser. Your birth date and time are never sent to a server, never stored, and never shared — the calculation runs on your own device and disappears the moment you close the page.

The science behind the numbers

Every figure is built from well-established population averages and astronomical constants. Real people vary — a calm heart beats slower, a restless sleeper dreams more — so treat these as a beautiful approximation rather than a medical measurement.

HeartbeatsBased on an average resting heart rate of about 72 beats per minute.
BreathsAround 16 breaths per minute for a typical adult at rest.
BlinksRoughly 15 blinks a minute, counted only while you're awake.
Hours of sleepHumans spend close to one third of their lives asleep.
Steps walkedAn everyday average of about 7,500 steps per day.
Full moonsOne full moon arrives every 29.53 days — the length of a lunar cycle.
Trips around the SunEarth completes one orbit a year: one lap for every birthday.
Distance through spaceOur solar system races around the galaxy at roughly 828,000 km/h, carrying you along for the ride.

Questions people ask

Are these numbers exact?

No — and that's part of the fun. They're friendly estimates based on human averages, so your real totals will differ. The goal is wonder and perspective, not precision.

Do you store my date of birth?

Never. The entire calculation happens in your browser. Nothing you type is uploaded, saved, or sent anywhere.

Why do you ask for my time of birth?

Only to make your "seconds alive" count a little more precise. It's completely optional — leave it blank and we'll simply assume midday.

Can I share my results?

Yes. You can post them straight to your favourite social network, or download your result as an image to share anywhere you like.

Is this medical or health advice?

Not at all. It's a playful way to look at the time you've lived — nothing here should be used for health decisions.
