About celestial names

Throughout history, humans have looked to the night sky for inspiration when naming their children. From ancient navigators who used stars to guide their journeys to modern astronomers who discover new celestial bodies, the heavens have provided us with beautiful, meaningful names.

This generator draws from:

  • Constellations - Patterns of stars that form mythological figures and animals
  • Individual stars - Named stellar bodies, many with Arabic or Greek origins
  • Planets & moons - Worlds in our solar system and beyond
  • Mythological figures - Gods, goddesses, and heroes associated with the cosmos
  • Astronomical terms - Scientific concepts and phenomena from astronomy

Each name suggestion includes a brief explanation of its celestial origin, helping you connect your child's name to the wonders of the universe.

How it works

Select your preferences for gender, name length, and number of suggestions. The generator will curate a list of names from our celestial database, complete with their astronomical origins and meanings.

Tips for choosing

Consider how the name sounds with your last name, any potential nicknames, and the story behind the name that resonates with you and your family's values.
